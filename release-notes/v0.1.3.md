# Simple Mac Keyboard Control 0.1.3

- Enable or disable individual physical displays from the menu on Apple silicon, including using an external display while keeping your MacBook open. The last usable display stays enabled; checkboxes are hidden when only one display is attached.
- Automatically restore app-disabled displays when quitting, after an app crash, or when the remaining display is unplugged.
- Remember software brightness across reconnects and app restarts for monitors with a usable, unique hardware serial identity.
- Fix missing menu-bar icons after display reconnection by avoiding unnecessary brightness panels and letting display changes settle before restoring dimming.

Available for Apple silicon and Intel in stable and beta app identities. Display switching requires Apple silicon and independently identifiable physical displays; mirrored and ambiguous configurations are not switchable. Existing audio and brightness functionality remains available on Intel.

Cable recovery, remembered dimming, and menu-bar restoration were physically verified with a MacBook and LG FULL HD display on macOS 27 beta. Sleep/wake and broader hardware coverage remain pending. Automated release checks cover native builds, signing, notarization, and Sparkle upgrades on both architectures.
